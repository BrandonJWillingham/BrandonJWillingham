## Featured Projects

### 🌿 Herbalur

A production-focused full-stack e-commerce platform developed for a natural skincare, haircare, and wellness brand.

**Built with:** Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Prisma, PostgreSQL, Zustand, Stripe, Shippo, Resend, and Vercel.

Features include:

- Custom storefront and shopping cart
- Stripe checkout
- Product and inventory management
- Customer and order management
- Shipping and label generation
- Email capture and marketing integrations
- Custom administrative dashboard
- First-party visitor and behavioral analytics
